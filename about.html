Our story

Why Cross Dagger

A tool without a plan is just weight. Here is where the name, the mark and the way we work come from.

Two is one.
One is none.

A dagger is a tool, but a tool without a plan is just weight. In Special Forces we say two is one, and one is none. That is not about carrying more gear. It is about planning for the moment something fails and knowing what you will do next. Cross Dagger exists to give your organization both: the right tools, and the plan that tells your people when and how to use them.

The name also keeps me close to my roots. De Oppresso Liber, to free the oppressed, has stayed close to my heart through my years in Special Forces. At its core, that work was about helping people. Cross Dagger carries it forward.

I grew up in a small village in American Samoa where everything was scarce and we made do with what we had. Since then I have made decisions by thinking through the first, second and third order effects. Without the right tools to plan, it is hard to see those second and third order effects, let alone choose the best ones. That is why I find the threats and vulnerabilities in a system, then build or rebuild it to be as strong as it can be.

I have watched organizations fail under pressure for the same reasons: nobody thought to prepare, nobody brought the right tools, and nobody could counter what the adversary brought. They showed up with a fork and a spoon when they needed a full kit. Military planning taught me the opposite. Plan for the worst case and the most likely one. Build contingencies. Know your decision points, the moments after which there is no going back and the mission will either succeed or fail.

I started Cross Dagger to help organizations, and the people in them, build themselves into something greater. I believe in people. My job is to make sure you are ready when it counts.

The mark. The dagger is the tool. The interlocked links mean two is one, one is none. The crossed arrows honor where I came from and remind us that threats come from every direction.

Marcus LolofieFounder, Cross Dagger Solutions
