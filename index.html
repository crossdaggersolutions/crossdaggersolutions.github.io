1.Situation

Most gaps surface in the middle of a disruption

Three questions every leadership team should be able to answer before something goes wrong. If any of them causes a pause, that pause is where we start.

Q1

What stops first?

If a key person, site, system or supplier is lost tomorrow, which services fail, and in what order?

Q2

How long can you tolerate it?

At what point does downtime turn into serious financial, safety, legal or reputational damage?

Q3

Who decides?

Does everyone know who has authority, what the first moves are, and how the team communicates when normal channels are down?

2.Mission

4.Sustainment

How we protect you during and after the work

What you can expect

Fixed scope, in writing

Sites, interviews, deliverables and review rounds agreed before work starts. Changes are priced and approved first.

One sponsor, clear decisions

You name one accountable sponsor. Every briefing ends with decisions and owners.

Honest boundaries

Penetration testing, engineering and legal advice are outside our lane. We tell you when you need a specialist.

How we handle your information

  • A mutual NDA is signed before any site visit or sensitive discussion.
  • Access is limited to the people working your engagement.
  • Your information does not go into AI tools unless you approve it in writing.
  • When the engagement ends, your material is returned or destroyed, or kept only where records laws or professional obligations require it.
  • Please do not email site plans or security details. We set up a secure transfer at kickoff.
