The problem it solves

Risk posture drifts. Sites change, people leave, suppliers consolidate, and the threats you planned for last year may not be the ones that matter now. Without a current, scored picture, leaders end up spending on what is visible instead of what is likely.

A risk assessment plans for the most likely threat and the worst case, shows what has changed, and tells you what to fix first.

How it works

  1. Discover and scopeMutual NDA, a readiness check, and a fixed scope and fee in writing. Sites, interviews, deliverables and review rounds are agreed before work starts.
  2. AssessSite review, interviews, and threat and vulnerability data across physical, personnel and operational risk.
  3. AnalyzeA scored risk register with dependencies and gaps.
  4. PlanA prioritized mitigation plan with owners.
  5. Brief and decideA leadership briefing that ends in decisions, not a report that sits on a shelf.
